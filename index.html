ABOUT THE DATA

One player. One primary position.

The pool covers Moroccan-nationality players, including dual nationals, and is not limited to current senior call-ups. Players are assigned to one primary position only; no player appears in more than one position list. The two centre-back and defensive-midfield slots share their corresponding primary-position lists, but the formation displays different players in each slot. Each list contains the ten highest-valued names in that primary-position group. Values are estimates, not transfer fees or performance ratings. This is a dated snapshot, not a live feed.
