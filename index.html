ABOUT THIS VIEW

A depth chart, not a team sheet.

Moroccan Footballers maps the senior player pool into a 4-2-3-1. Position assignments are tactical interpretations, not official position labels. Versatile players may appear in more than one list. “Latest squad” refers to the 26-man selection announced on 4 November 2026; “Recent call-up” refers to players in the 17 September 2026 squad who were not in that November group.
